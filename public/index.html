How to Download

Four easy steps.

1

Copy the link

In Instagram open the Reel, tap Share and choose Copy link.

2

Paste it here

Paste the link in the box above, or tap the Paste button.

3

Preview

Press Download. We check the link and show a preview of the video.

4

Save MP4

Tap Download MP4. On iPhone tap Save Video and choose Save Video / Save to Files.
